# H14 — Evolve Programs with LLM Mutation
_parent: **Search a Population Against an Executable Fitness** (`population_search_executable_fitness`) · corpus support: 31 papers (accepted 9 · rejected 11 · unlabeled 11)_

## Tactical pattern
The genome is executable code: an algorithm, heuristic, priority function, reward function, control policy, action
filter, equation skeleton, solver operator, kernel, or serving policy. An LLM proposes edits to parents sampled from a
population, archive or set of islands; an automated evaluator runs each child; winners are retained. The decisive
design choices are (a) the size and placement of the evolved unit — small operators or skeletons inside a fixed,
validity-preserving scaffold, with numeric constants fitted numerically, rather than a monolithic end-to-end solver —
and (b) how evaluations are spent: novelty rejection of redundant mutations, exploration-aware parent sampling, bandit
choice of mutation model, budget allocation across lineages. Success is certified by the evaluator's score on
instances not used for selection, often reinforced by the fact that the output is inspectable code.

## Step-by-Step
1. **Build the evaluator first.** It must be automated, trusted and cheap enough to call many times. When it is
   expensive (policy training, simulation), use replay or proxy tasks, but check that proxy rankings transfer; proxy
   rankings that may not predict larger settings are a recurring member limitation.
2. **Shrink the evolved unit.** Evolve operators, priority functions or skeletons inside a scaffold that guarantees
   validity or monotone progress, and fit continuous parameters with a numerical optimizer.
3. **Ground mutations in evaluator traces.** Feed back training dynamics, legality violations or visual failure
   analysis instead of relying on the model's internal knowledge alone.
4. **Spend evaluations deliberately.** Filter near-duplicate children, sample parents with exploration in mind, and
   allocate budget across mutation models or lineages; report API or compute cost.
5. **Separate selection from reporting and report spread.** Hold out evaluation instances, report variance rather
   than a single-run best, and disclose human edits. Running a generic evolution loop in a new domain while selecting
   on supplied scores with unclear held-out separation is what sinks most rejected members.

## When to pick this one
The artifact runs, the space is irregular, and you can write a scorer that the proposer cannot easily game and that
returns within the budget. The tell is a problem where a better heuristic or program is the deliverable and its
quality is measurable by execution. If evaluations are so costly that discarding lessons between generations is
wasteful, move to H15; if partial winners complement each other on different instances, move to H16; if the lever
is wording rather than code, use H13.

## Tactical failure mode
The root assumption that fails is that a generic LLM-evolution loop transfers to a new domain unchanged and that the
evaluator score is itself the result. Most rejected members apply the loop to a new domain (query generation, routing,
exact solvers, code constructions, geospatial modelling, kernels, feature engineering, collective-action policies)
without a search-level contribution; 9 of the 11 rejected members carry no recorded limitations. The scaffold idea
alone does not decide outcome: evolving operators inside a fixed metaheuristic appears among both accepted and
rejected members, and the accepted instance reports variance, a matched budget and optimizer cost. Member limitations
add the other recurring weaknesses: headline numbers from single runs, final checkpoint selection on the task metric,
memorisation of familiar solutions, and executor failures that silently drop candidates.

## Lessons
### From accepted work
- Making the loop sample-efficient (novelty rejection, adaptive parent sampling, bandit choice of mutation model) is
  a contribution in its own right.
- Evolving small operators inside a fixed, monotone scaffold makes the search tractable — when paired with variance
  and budget reporting.
- Grounding edits in what the evaluator observed (training curves, illegal actions, visual failure analysis) gives
  mutations direction.
- Executable output is a feature: code rewards inferred from demonstrations, programmatic control policies and
  equation skeletons with fitted constants are inspectable as well as competitive.
- Research ideas can be scored by actually executing them in parallel rather than by plausibility judgements.
### From rejected work
- A generic evolution loop pointed at a new domain, with no search-level novelty, reads as an application note.
- Selecting on supplied scores without a clear held-out split leaves gains unverifiable.
- A single benchmark, with ambiguous meta-level results excluded, narrows the claim to one setting.
- Correctness-critical domains need explicit correctness gates in fitness; a framework description alone does not
  establish them.
- Changing a retrieval key or orchestration layer without isolating its effect leaves the mechanism unattributed.
