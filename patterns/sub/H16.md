# H16 — Preserve a Frontier of Complementary Candidates
_parent: **Search a Population Against an Executable Fitness** (`population_search_executable_fitness`) · corpus support: 13 papers (accepted 4 · rejected 1 · unlabeled 8)_

## Tactical pattern
Instead of keeping one incumbent, selection retains a set of mutually non-dominated or mutually different candidates:
a per-instance Pareto frontier, a cost-versus-utility or multi-objective frontier (pass rate versus inference cost,
attack strength versus realism), niches, quality-diversity cells over structural and efficiency descriptors,
router-conditioned prompt slots, or an elite bank indexed by modification surface and failure pathology. Globally
weaker candidates that win on some instances or objectives survive and can later be recombined, which counters
premature convergence and lets complementary strategies merge. The pattern is defined by the retention rule and
composes with any proposal mechanism — prompts, workflows, skills, harnesses, network code. The decisive design choice
is the axis of complementarity together with the gate that admits a candidate to the archive. Success is certified by
held-out gains plus evidence that retaining diversity, not just the best member, carries the improvement.

## Step-by-Step
1. **Name the axes of complementarity.** Per-instance wins, cost, realism, task type or failure pathology — whatever
   makes the best-on-average candidate lose on an identifiable subset.
2. **Pick the retention rule to match.** Per-instance Pareto for heterogeneous examples, nondominated sorting for
   explicit trade-offs, niches or quality-diversity cells for structural variety, routed slots for task streams.
3. **Gate archive entry.** Require validity, evidence that the edit actually activates, paired significance or a
   higher-fidelity confirmation, and frozen tolerances on capabilities that must not regress; without gates, inactive
   or noisy benchmark-specific edits get promoted.
4. **Recombine with restraint.** Keep the number of parents per recombination small (many parents can overwhelm LLM
   recombination) and budget for the larger population's cost.
5. **Validate on a split disjoint from selection and ablate the retention rule.** Selection and reporting sharing
   tasks, and recombination without a controlled ablation, are recorded limitations of unlabeled members.

## Differentiation within parent
- **vs H13 / H14 / H15:** those siblings are defined by the proposal mechanism (undirected prompt variation, LLM code
  mutation, distilled memory); H16 is defined by what survives selection and can sit on top of any of them.
- Within the bundle, H16 members span prompts, workflows, skill bundles, harnesses and network code, whereas H13 is
  confined to prompt-like text and H14 to executable code.

## When to pick this one
Inputs are heterogeneous, objectives conflict, or greedy search keeps collapsing — local gains regress other tasks,
or one evolving prompt suffers interference across task streams. The tell is a best-so-far candidate that wins on
average but loses on identifiable subsets, or a cost axis the scalar score ignores. For a single objective over a
homogeneous task, plain elitism inside H13 or H14 is simpler; when the problem is discarded lessons rather than
discarded candidates, use H15.

## Tactical failure mode
The root assumption that fails is that preserved diversity is useful diversity and that the frontier axes are
measured reliably. Members record the cracks: pathology labels that can be misdiagnosed, single-episode champion
probes that misalign with final performance, best-only broadcast that sacrifices the diversity it was meant to keep,
validation that consumes most rollouts, larger populations that raise per-query cost, and dependence on informative
feedback. The single rejected member treats diversity as the end product — selecting a complementary set of
conditioned agents to represent a population — with no executable fitness loop around it.

## Lessons
### From accepted work
- Per-instance Pareto selection over trace-reflected instructions keeps complementary strategies alive and escapes
  the local optima of greedy prompt optimization — while honestly noting that validation consumes most rollouts.
- A frontier archive over conflicting objectives, fed by critic-filtered, evaluator-routed proposals, avoids wasting
  an expensive simulator on implausible candidates.
- Jointly evolving a router and specialised prompt slots removes destructive interference across heterogeneous tasks.
- Selecting a frontier of prompts can be framed as multi-objective pure exploration with formal identification
  guarantees.
- All 3 accepted members with an evidence block report held-out separation, and 2 of them ablate the retained
  structure.
### From rejected work
- Optimizing a diverse set as the deliverable, with no executable fitness loop, falls outside what this pattern buys.
- Submodular selection for representativeness optimizes coverage, not task performance, so retained diversity has
  no fitness to be certified against.
