## ‡ numbers (computed by us)
- 1.96‡ (blogs only): the printed 95% intervals match mean +- 1.96 x SD / sqrt(5): AEL 2.13 +- 1.96 x 0.47/sqrt(5) = [1.72, 2.54]; EvoTool 1.37 +- 1.96 x 1.74/sqrt(5) = [-0.16, 2.90]. A t interval with 4 degrees of freedom would be wider. This is our check of how the paper's interval was built; the paper does not state the formula. (The string 1.96 also occurs in the paper as an unrelated Table 14 value.)
- Not decimals, but our inferences: at a Bonferroni threshold of 0.05/9 the Reflexion Welch result (p = .008) would not pass; the paper mentions no multiplicity correction. Every other decimal number in the blogs and on the poster is printed in the paper. Counts such as "three of nine" (comparisons with Welch p < .05) are read off Table 5, not computed.

## Notes
- Table 2a "best" in the poster title means best mean on Sharpe, Sortino and Calmar; the best maximum drawdown is EvoTool (-1.46, bold in the table); equal-weight (-1.47) also beats AEL (-1.53).
- Support-ticket routing: Table 2b lists "Fixed policy / Class-balanced" 0.565 +- 0.036 and "No memory / Stateless" 0.512 +- 0.010 in separate rows above the "Prior" group; the strongest "Prior" row is FactorMiner 0.557. The abstract's "+51%" and the introduction's "+28.3pp" compare AEL with FactorMiner.
- The "last regime only" row of the poster table uses R3 from Table 2b (AEL 0.885, TS without reflection 0.685, best prior ExpeL 0.705). The paper's own deterministic variant (Table 11, no LLM calls) shows R3 0.863 against 0.206.
- Synthetic bandit (Table 4, Appendix U): the hidden 11th arm is injected by a scripted rule (trailing 25-episode mean below 0.42), no LLM. "Best other" 0.410 is UCB1's R3 value in Table 4.
- 20Newsgroups: the text names fixed "compressed" (0.267) as the strongest fixed policy, whereas Table 10 lists fixed "full_detailed" at 0.271 +- 0.022; the poster shows the table value as "best other".
- Appendix A.2: the shuffled-reflection variant is described as an additional 5-seed run; the paper reports no significance test for it. The reflection-faithfulness numbers (40/49 = 82%, judge scores) are from one seed (42) and are only in the blogs.
- Internal inconsistencies, reported in the blogs and on the poster with the table value used: HyperAgent 0.72 +- 0.38 (Table 2a, Section 4.2) vs 0.46 +- 0.41 (Appendix K, Table 16, Section K.1); LLM-FCC credit 1.37 (Section 4.3 and Figure 3a caption) vs 1.49 (Table 3 and Figure 4) vs -0.53 after a credit-code fix (Appendices H and J); FCC 1.04 (Table 3) vs -0.07 post-fix; "4 of 9 comparisons survive" (Section 4.6) names three baselines and Table 5 shows three with p < .05; Appendix E says the matched incremental build uses seeds 42, 123, 456 while the Table 2 caption says N = 5 for all stochastic methods; the abstract says AEL has "the lowest variance among all stochastic methods" while Table 2a lists HyperAgent at 0.38 vs AEL 0.47 (Figure 3 caption restricts the claim to LLM methods with mean Sharpe above 1.0); the Table 7 caption refers to 3-seed pilot numbers without SD although every row shows an SD. Appendix B ("Public-results clarification") says the same configuration name can take different values across result blocks.
- Appendix H states the credit-assignment results of Table 14 predate credit-code fixes; Table 3 is stated to be post-fix. We use Table 3.
- Not checked by the paper (stated as "not reported" in the blogs): budget-matched baseline runs, tuning budgets of baselines other than HyperAgent, baseline costs, any test of a retained artifact on a different task, any leakage test beyond the argument that all LLM methods share the Claude Haiku 4.5 backbone (Appendix B).
- Cost: Appendix E gives about $2.80 per full AEL run (about $2 predictions, $0.80 reflection and evolution). Table 16 separately labels about 400 LLM calls (AEL) and about 20 (HyperAgent) as "Evolution cost"; the two figures are not presented as the same quantity. Transaction costs (Appendix R, Table 22): headline numbers are at 0 bp; at 20 bp the paper gives approximate (~) values AEL 1.3, momentum 1.0, EvoTool 0.7.
- New retrieval-policy arms: Section 3.4 gives the syntax-validation + smoke-test gate for LLM-written planner classes; for retrieval policies Appendix P.2 says only that both example artifacts "were validated via AST parsing and accepted into the module pool by LinUCB" (LinUCB is otherwise described only for planner selection). The poster says "AST-checked in App P.2". The paper gives no accept/reject rule beyond this; we do not claim one.
- HyperAgent: Appendix K describes a tuned version (20 generations, real tool schemas, epsilon acceptance, temperature 0.7) with 0.46 +- 0.41 over 5 seeds (Table 16; Section J.1 says 0.46); Tables 2a and 5 show 0.72 +- 0.38. The paper does not say which version the Table 2a row is.
- Routing: in the LLM-agent run (Table 2b) the bandit without reflection scores 0.685 on R3 and ExpeL 0.705; AEL improves R1/R2/R3 from 0.590/0.585/0.685 to 0.775/0.715/0.885 (Appendix A.5: "not only in the route-aware regime"). The 0.206 -> 0.863 pattern is the deterministic stream (Table 11, no LLM calls), whose caption says the route-aware policy is not in the initial pool.
- Finance arm injection: only the seed-42 trace (Section 3.5, Appendix S) and a seed-123 example (Appendix P.2); firing rate, finance slow-window size and per-seed AEL Sharpe (main configuration) are not given; no ablation separates injected arms from the insight text.
- Table 6/7: best fixed policies on training reward compressed 0.523 and recent_window 0.514 are above Thompson Sampling 0.490; none + reflection 1.41 vs Stateless 1.35; +Memory 1.68 above every fixed-policy + reflection variant (best 1.44). Appendix A.2 text calls shuffled reflection (1.54) "marginally better" than +Memory (1.68); 1.54 is lower.
- Authors: header order follows the PDF title block (Wujiang Xu, Jiaojiao Han, Minghao Guo, Kai Mei, Xi Zhu, Han Zhang, Dimitris N. Metaxas; Rutgers University). Plain-text extraction of the PDF orders them differently.
- Seeds: "matched incremental build uses seeds 42, 123, 456" appears in Section 4.1 and Appendix E; the Table 2 caption says N = 5.
- Poster ablation panel shows selected rows of Table 3 (omits cold-start 0.82, skill extraction 1.02, FCC 1.04, LLM-FCC 1.49) plus the shuffled-reflection row from Appendix A.2 (flagged on the poster).

## Judgment notes
- `optimizer_cost: unclear` (batch b18). The full paper reports the cost of an AEL run (Appendix E: about $2.80 per run; Table 16: about 400 LLM calls vs about 20 for HyperAgent), though not baseline costs. This looks like evidence the condensed text cut; condition S4 ("search cost on the table") is then arguably met.
- `cross_task: unclear` is consistent with the full paper under the EVIDENCE_SCHEMA definition: the routing, synthetic and 20Newsgroups streams are separate benchmarks on which AEL is run again from scratch; no retained artifact is evaluated on a different task. The paper itself calls them mechanism checks.
- F4 is recorded as `yes` with the quote "On mean Sharpe, 4 of 9 comparisons survive (Reflexion, Meta-Reflexion, HyperAgent)". The quote is inconsistent with Table 5, which shows three comparisons with p < .05; the direction of the judgment (few seeds, large per-seed variance, mean differences against the strongest baselines not significant) is supported by the full paper.
